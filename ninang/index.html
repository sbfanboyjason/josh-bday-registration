    Questions

    Is there a minimum amount?

    No. Any amount from the heart is welcome, and so are items.

    When will my name appear on the wall?

    Once our team has checked your GCash payment or received your items. We email you when that happens.

    Can I give without my name being shown?

    Yes. Choose Anonymous Ninang. We still keep your details for our records, but your name is not shown on the wall or in shoutouts.

    What items are helpful?

    Loot bag items (125 or more pieces so everyone gets one), prizes for the game rounds, and food or drinks. If you are not sure, message us first.

    Is this an official SB19 event?

    No. GAME TIME is organized by fans, SB19 A'tin Fanboys, and is not an official SB19 or management event.

    Who do I contact?

    Email sbfanboyjason@gmail.com or message our Facebook page. Include your Ninang ID if you have one.
